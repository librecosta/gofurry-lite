## Discover the Furry Web with GoFurry Lite

The furry fandom is much bigger than any single website, social network, or game. Somewhere out there is an artist sharing their latest character design, a writer publishing a story, a small community welcoming new members, or an independent developer building a world inhabited by anthropomorphic animals. The interesting part is finding these places.

**GoFurry Lite is a furry website directory and furry game discovery guide** built to make that exploration easier. Instead of sending you through endless search results, it brings together furry communities, art galleries, literature, creative resources, conventions, commissions, and games in one place.

Whether you have been part of the fandom for years or have only just discovered it, there is always another corner of the furry web worth exploring.

### Why does the furry web need its own directory?

Try searching for a furry website you visited years ago. Perhaps you remember its logo, a character from its homepage, or just a few words from its name. The original site might still exist, but finding it among social media profiles, unrelated search results, and abandoned links can be surprisingly difficult.

That is one reason GoFurry exists.

Furry communities have never lived entirely on one platform. Forums, personal galleries, creator portfolios, species reference pages, convention websites, independent projects, and game communities have all contributed to the fandom's online history. Some are widely known; others are small projects maintained by only a handful of people.

Search engines are useful, but they do not always explain what a website actually does or how it relates to the furry fandom. A categorized directory provides another way to explore: **start with an interest, discover a website, learn what it offers, and decide where to go next.**

GoFurry Lite continues that idea with a simpler interface, familiar historical links, and a growing opportunity to keep useful information accessible.

### What kinds of furry websites can I find here?

There is no single way to experience the furry fandom. Some people come for the artwork, others for stories, games, friends, creative tools, conventions, or the excitement of bringing an original character to life.

That is why GoFurry Lite organizes websites into eight practical categories.

If meeting other fans is your starting point, explore [furry communities](/categories/community). Forums, discussion spaces, and community websites can lead to conversations about original characters, artwork, roleplay, game projects, and local events.

For visual inspiration, visit [Art & Galleries](/categories/gallery). Furry art websites and creator portfolios offer different approaches to character illustration, digital painting, photography, and personal projects. If your interests lean toward storytelling, [Literature](/categories/literature) is where to begin looking for furry fiction, original novels, and fan-created writing.

Artists and makers may find [Tools & Resources](/categories/resources) useful for tutorials, reference material, and creative utilities. Meanwhile, [Commissions & Marketplace](/categories/marketplace) brings together destinations connected to art commissions, fursuit makers, custom creations, and furry merchandise.

Curious about the fandom itself? [News & Knowledge](/categories/knowledge) points toward information resources, reference websites, and community-related reading. [Games & Interactive](/categories/gaming) covers game project websites and interactive communities, while [Events & Organizations](/categories/events) offers a path toward furry conventions, meetups, and organized community activities.

You can scroll through every available entry inside each homepage category card, or open a dedicated category page when you want descriptions and search tools.

The categories are starting points, not rigid boundaries. A creator's website may be both an art portfolio and a place to accept commissions. A convention website may also be a community hub. Follow the connections that interest you.

### New to the furry fandom? Start wherever you feel curious.

You do not need an elaborate fursona, a collection of furry artwork, or a convention ticket to start exploring.

The furry fandom brings together people who enjoy anthropomorphic animal characters in art, stories, costumes, animation, games, and other forms of creative expression. Some fans design original characters; others enjoy the work of artists, writers, musicians, or game developers.

If you want to understand the creative side first, browse furry galleries and literature. If you are looking for a conversation, try community websites. If you prefer learning about events and how the fandom gathers in person, explore conventions and organizations.

You can also start with a familiar interest. Someone who enjoys role-playing games might discover a furry visual novel. An illustrator could find character references or a fursuit maker's portfolio. A reader might stumble upon a serialized story hosted on a small independent website.

**There is no required route through the fandom.** A good directory should make exploration easier without telling you which corner you ought to enjoy.

### Where can I find furry games, visual novels, and indie projects?

Furry games deserve their own space because they can be just as varied as the fandom itself.

Some feature anthropomorphic protagonists in adventure games or RPGs. Others explore character-driven storytelling through furry visual novels, interactive fiction, puzzle games, platformers, rhythm games, or independent experiments that do not fit neatly into a single genre.

GoFurry Lite maintains a separate [furry game directory](/games) with historical game profiles, descriptions, genre and species tags, available screenshots, and links to storefronts such as Steam.

Looking for a specific title? Search by its name or use the available filters to narrow the directory. If you prefer discovering something unexpected, the homepage's Discover Games section presents a rotating selection of works from the available catalogue.

You can also browse [curated furry game collections](/games/collections). Collections help connect games through shared subjects or styles, such as visual novels, adventure experiences, particular gameplay genres, or titles historically grouped as free games.

Keep in mind that a game's price, availability, release status, and storefront information may have changed since its record was archived. A collection is a discovery aid, not a guarantee that a game is currently free, available, or compatible with your device.

And if you are looking for a game's official website rather than its catalogue profile, the [Games & Interactive website category](/categories/gaming) may lead you to related project pages and communities.

### Looking for furry conventions, fursuit makers, or artists for commissions?

Finding a furry convention and finding a fursuit maker are very different tasks, but both often begin with the same question: *Where is the right website?*

Our [Events & Organizations directory](/categories/events) helps you explore furry conventions, local gatherings, clubs, and other organized activities. A historical listing may help identify an event or organizer even when you no longer remember its exact name.

Before making travel plans, always check the organizer's current website for dates, venue information, registration details, accessibility, and age requirements. An archived event listing should never be mistaken for a confirmed upcoming schedule.

For fursuit commissions, character artwork, custom accessories, and related creations, explore [Commissions & Marketplace](/categories/marketplace). Creator websites can show portfolios, examples of previous work, ordering information, and the kinds of projects a maker accepts.

If you are considering a commission, read the creator's current terms, prices, queue status, payment conditions, and refund policies before sending money. GoFurry Lite does not process purchases or act as an intermediary between creators and customers.

These independent creators and organizers are an important part of the furry web. Giving their websites a discoverable place matters, even when the destination is small.

### How are websites and games organized on GoFurry Lite?

A long list of links is not very useful if you cannot tell what you are about to open.

GoFurry Lite combines category navigation with individual website and game profiles. A website entry can provide its name, description, logo, category, and a direct external link when a suitable destination is available. Game profiles can include available descriptions, tags, media, and storefront information.

The directory is based on the original GoFurry catalogue, reconstructed from a database snapshot dated **November 15, 2026**. Historical website IDs, game IDs, selected groupings, and public release notes have been preserved wherever practical.

You can still explore the [legacy website groups](/site-groups) and read the original site's [update archive](/updates). They provide another path through the directory and help preserve context around older links and projects.

The current catalogue is not a live website-health monitor, a universal quality ranking, or a guarantee that every external destination still works. Listings and their order should not be interpreted as a security certification or paid endorsement.

What we want to preserve is something more useful over time: an understandable map of websites, creative work, and the connections between them.

### How do I browse furry websites more safely?

Discovering new corners of the internet is enjoyable, but unfamiliar websites deserve the same common sense you would use anywhere online.

Before downloading software, creating an account, joining a community, or making a purchase, take a moment to check where you have landed.

Keep your browser and operating system updated. Be cautious about unexpected downloads, suspicious browser extensions, imitation login pages, and messages that pressure you into acting immediately. If a website asks for sensitive information, make sure you understand who operates it and why the information is needed.

For conventions, commissions, or online shops, use the organizer's or creator's current official information rather than relying entirely on an archived directory entry.

GoFurry Lite provides links and historical descriptions; it does **not** independently guarantee that third-party websites are malware-free, trustworthy, or suitable for every visitor.

It is also worth remembering that every external website has its own privacy practices. Following a link takes you outside GoFurry Lite and into another service's rules.

A little caution goes a long way toward making online exploration more enjoyable.

### What is the difference between SFW and NSFW mode?

The furry fandom includes work intended for different audiences, and not every visitor wants to encounter the same kind of material.

GoFurry Lite starts in **SFW mode**, which displays entries classified for the general directory. Switching to **NSFW mode** expands the visible catalogue to include adult-marked websites and games.

Your choice is stored as a browser preference, so it can remain in effect when you return or switch languages.

The labels come from available catalogue information and editorial decisions. They may not perfectly describe everything on an external website, particularly when that website changes over time. The NSFW switch is a content-display preference, **not an age-verification system**.

Whatever mode you choose, take notice of the destination's own content notices and applicable rules.

### Can I still find the original GoFurry, and why is there a Lite version?

The original GoFurry directory grew into a larger project with more features, while a new generation of [GoFurry](https://gofurry.com) is being developed separately.

GoFurry Lite keeps the older directory accessible without rebuilding its entire backend infrastructure. Its pages are statically generated, and browsing the catalogue does not require an account.

That simplicity is intentional. It helps preserve familiar pages while leaving room for the newer platform to evolve on its own.

In the future, public information from the new GoFurry may help keep Lite's catalogue updated. For now, historical records remain part of what makes this website useful.

### Found a broken link or a furry website we should know about?

A directory cannot discover every independent project, and websites rarely stay unchanged forever.

Perhaps an artist has moved their portfolio. Maybe a convention has a new domain, a game has changed its store page, or a small community deserves a place in the directory. You might also notice an incorrect description, a missing logo, or a classification that needs another look.

You can report these issues and suggest corrections through [GoFurry Lite on GitHub](https://github.com/librecosta/gofurry-lite/issues).

If you operate a furry website yourself, a clear description of what your site offers and its official address can help us understand a suggestion. Inclusion is not automatic, and we cannot promise a specific placement or ranking.

Sharing a useful directory with a friend, bookmarking a creator's page, or recommending a good independent project can also help people discover more of the furry web.

### A few more questions before you explore

#### Can I search for a furry website if I only remember part of its name?

Yes. Try the homepage search with the part you remember, a relevant word, or something from the site's description. To search for individual games instead, visit the [game directory](/games).

#### Where should I look for furry art, writing, or original characters?

Begin with [Art & Galleries](/categories/gallery) and [Literature](/categories/literature). Creator websites often offer more context about a project than a single image or post shared elsewhere.

#### Are all the games on GoFurry Lite available on Steam?

The game directory preserves historical Steam identifiers and storefront references where available. Check the linked store for current availability, supported platforms, pricing, and regional restrictions.

#### Can I use GoFurry Lite on my phone?

Yes. The directory is designed for desktop and mobile browsing. Category lists, site profiles, game cards, search, and filters adapt to smaller screens.

#### Why does a listing sometimes have no working visit link?

Some older records do not have a confirmed external destination. The profile may remain available to preserve the site's name, description, and history without sending you to an unverified address.

#### Is GoFurry Lite the same as the new GoFurry?

No. Lite focuses on preserving and presenting the original directory in a lightweight format. The [new GoFurry](https://gofurry.com) is a separate platform with its own direction.

### There is always another corner to discover

The furry web is made of more than the biggest communities and most recognizable games. It is also made of independent artists, little-known stories, personal websites, creators building things in their spare time, and organizers bringing people together.

GoFurry Lite cannot capture everything. But it can help keep those paths visible, one website and one game at a time.

Find somewhere interesting. Follow a link. Discover a creator you have never heard of. And perhaps leave with one more corner of the furry web worth remembering.
