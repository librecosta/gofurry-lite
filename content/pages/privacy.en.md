# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 21, 2026**

GoFurry Lite is a statically generated directory. It does not require registration or login, does not proxy traffic to external websites, and does not intentionally request sensitive personal information.

This policy explains the data involved in using the website.

## Information We Do Not Request

GoFurry Lite does not provide user accounts or profile management. We do not ask visitors to submit real names, identity documents, payment details, passwords, private messages, or precise locations.

If you contact us through GitHub, information you submit is handled according to GitHub's services and privacy practices.

## Browsing and Search

Directory pages are delivered as static content. Website searches, game searches, and content filters operate in your browser.

GoFurry Lite does not require a personal account to use these features and does not operate the original website's user-facing monitoring and popularity-tracking services.

## Local Preferences

Some settings are stored in your browser's local storage, including your preferred color theme and SFW/NSFW display mode.

These settings help preserve your preferences between visits. They are not a user account and are not intended to identify you.

You can clear them using your browser's website-data controls.

## Hosting, Cache, and Basic Logs

GoFurry Lite is delivered through Cloudflare. As part of providing and protecting the website, the hosting and network infrastructure may process technical request information such as IP addresses, request times, and browser or network details.

Browser caching and network caching may also be used to improve delivery performance and reliability.

GoFurry Lite does not operate a separate Redis-based user-tracking service.

## Third-Party Resources and Links

Some game images and media are served from Steam's content delivery infrastructure. Loading these resources may result in requests to Steam-operated services.

Following an external website or game-store link takes you to a third-party service, which may use its own cookies, accounts, analytics, and privacy practices.

Please review the relevant third-party policies when using those services.

## Contact and Corrections

If you believe a listing contains incorrect personal information, infringes privacy or copyright, or requires correction or removal, please contact us through [GoFurry Lite GitHub Issues](https://github.com/librecosta/gofurry-lite/issues).

We will review the information provided and consider appropriate corrections, restrictions, or removal.
