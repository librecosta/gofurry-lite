# About GoFurry Lite

*A familiar place to discover the furry web.*

## A place worth keeping

GoFurry began as a directory dedicated to furry culture: a place to discover communities, independent websites, creative projects, games, and the people behind them.

Over time, the original website grew into something more ambitious, with additional services and tools for exploring the furry web. As GoFurry moves toward a new platform, we wanted to preserve what made the original directory useful: its collection of websites, game records, curated categories, and familiar links.

That is why **GoFurry Lite** exists.

Lite is a smaller, independent continuation of the original GoFurry directory. It keeps the focus on finding interesting places and works without bringing back the complexity of the previous platform.

## What you can discover

GoFurry Lite brings together furry-related websites across eight categories, including communities, galleries, literature, tools, marketplaces, knowledge resources, games, and events.

You can also explore a collection of furry-themed games, browse curated game collections, and revisit public release notes from the original website.

Every listing is intended to be a useful starting point. When you find something interesting, you can read its profile or follow a link to the original website or game store.

## Preserving the original directory

The initial Lite catalogue was reconstructed from a snapshot of the original GoFurry databases dated November 15, 2026.

We have retained historical identifiers and important page addresses wherever possible, so existing links to websites and games can continue to lead somewhere meaningful.

Some information reflects the state of the original records and may no longer describe a third-party website or game as it exists today. We aim to correct and improve these records over time rather than present historical information as live observations.

## Built to stay simple

GoFurry Lite is a statically generated website. Its pages are prepared in advance and delivered without requiring the original GoFurry backend or a user account system.

Search and filtering run in your browser. You can switch between light and dark themes, choose English or Simplified Chinese, and control whether mature listings appear in the directory.

The SFW/NSFW switch is a display preference, not an age-verification system or a guarantee about the content of external websites.

## GoFurry continues

Lite preserves the original directory experience, while the next generation of GoFurry is being developed separately.

The new platform is available at [gofurry.com](https://gofurry.com).

As that platform matures, it may become a source of updated public information for Lite. The two websites have different purposes, but share the goal of making furry culture easier to discover.

## Feedback and contributions

Directories are never perfect. Websites change addresses, projects evolve, and information becomes outdated.

If you notice an incorrect link, a missing project, a description that needs correction, or a listing that raises copyright or privacy concerns, you are welcome to contact us through GitHub.

- [GoFurry Lite on GitHub](https://github.com/librecosta/gofurry-lite)
- [Report an issue or suggest a correction](https://github.com/librecosta/gofurry-lite/issues)
- [Explore the new GoFurry](https://gofurry.com)

Thank you for being part of the furry web and helping keep its many corners discoverable.
